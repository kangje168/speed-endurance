Last updated: October 13, 2026

## What the app processes

Speed Endurance uses your Garmin watch's location and speed information to determine whether your running speed meets your selected target pace. It records target paces, interval durations, stop reasons, dates, subsequent rest durations, and personal bests on the watch.

The app also creates a standard FIT running activity. Depending on your device and Garmin's recording behavior, this activity may contain your GPS route, timing, speed, distance, and other device-supplied activity metrics, such as heart rate.

## Where data is stored and shared

Interval history and personal bests are stored locally in the app's storage on your watch. The app keeps the latest 40 intervals and stores best records separately.

FIT activity files are saved in your watch's activity storage. Garmin software may synchronize them to your Garmin Connect account according to your settings. Garmin's handling of that data is governed by [Garmin's privacy policy](https://www.garmin.com/privacy/connect/).

The app does not send data to the publisher or a developer-operated server. It includes no advertising, analytics SDK, or in-app account system.

## Permissions

- **Positioning:** provides location quality and speed information for outdoor pace tracking.
- **Activity recording (Fit):** lets the app start, pause, and save a running activity on the watch.

## Your choices and data deletion

You can stop an interval or save and exit using the watch buttons. Removing the app through Garmin's app-management tools removes its app-local storage according to Garmin's device behavior. Saved FIT activities and activities already synchronized to Garmin Connect are separate from app-local history; manage or delete them through the watch and Garmin Connect.

Deleting an app-local record does not delete a FIT activity or a copy already synchronized to Garmin. Garmin account settings control Garmin Connect sharing and synchronization.

## Support email

If you email support, the publisher receives the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request. Avoid sending private activity files or location data unless needed for troubleshooting. Contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention requirements.
