## Full description

How long can you sustain your chosen running pace?

Speed Endurance helps you practice holding a target pace across repeated running intervals. Choose from 12 pace levels, from 5:30/km to 2:45/km in 15-second steps.

Press START once GPS is ready. When your speed falls below the target, a 60-second countdown begins. Reaching the target again resets the countdown. If you remain below the target for 60 continuous seconds, the app stops the interval timer and alerts you with vibration and sound, where available.

Rest timing begins automatically. You receive one reminder after three minutes of rest. At five minutes, the app saves the workout and exits. To continue sooner, press START during rest to begin the next interval. You can also choose a different target pace between intervals.

Features:

- 12 target paces, from 5:30/km to 2:45/km.
- Running timer, instantaneous pace, and below-target countdown.
- Automatic interval stopping after 60 continuous seconds below target.
- Automatic rest timing, a reminder after three minutes, and save-and-exit at five minutes.
- A separate longest-interval personal best for each pace level.
- Local history for the latest 40 intervals, including rest durations.
- A FIT running activity with a lap for each interval; recording pauses during rest.
- No ads, in-app account, subscription, or developer-operated server.

Record rules:

An interval's recorded duration runs from START until the interval stops. It includes the final 60 seconds below target. To qualify for a personal best, the interval must reach the target at least once and must have no detected GPS/data interruptions. Manually stopped intervals can qualify too.

GPS behavior:

This app requires usable GPS data to start and is intended for outdoor running. Zero speed with valid GPS counts as below target. Missing, poor, or stale GPS data resets the below-target countdown and prevents that interval from setting a personal best. The app uses instantaneous device speed; GPS fluctuations can reset the countdown when speed reaches the target. This version is not designed for indoor treadmill sessions.

Controls:

- START / STOP: start an interval, end the current interval, or start the next interval during rest.
- UP / DOWN: change the target pace when not running, or browse history.
- Hold UP: open or close history.
- BACK / LAP: end the current interval while running; save and exit during rest or pace selection; return from history.

Privacy:

Training history and personal bests are stored on your watch. FIT activities may sync to Garmin Connect through Garmin's normal synchronization, according to your settings. The app itself does not send data to the publisher or any third-party server. See the linked privacy policy for details.
